How to search (tap ✕ Tips to close)

  • Most lost dogs are found within 1 to 2 miles (the inner rings). Scared dogs hide in the day and move at dusk, night and dawn.
  • Downhill first: on steep ground they follow hollows, creeks, trails and power-line cuts down toward the valley (East Valley Rd, Dunlap). Use the Topo layer: blue lines are creeks, tight lines are steep.
  • Do not call her or walk at her. Sit or crouch, look away, toss treats, let her come.
  • Leave water + a worn shirt or her bed at the last-seen spot and at the bottom of the bluff.
  • Ask people outside, check barns, porches, sheds, culverts, under decks.
  • Call Sequatchie County and Rhea County animal control and nearby vets; post in local Facebook lost-pet groups.
  • Drone tonight: the green circle is 0.5 mile around where she was last seen. Move it to the newest sighting if one comes in.
